# Kiln first-impression test

You are shown only the first screen of a website's home page, on a desktop and
on a phone, exactly as a visitor sees it before scrolling or clicking. You have
not seen any other part of the site and must not look at any other file.

Answer from these two images alone, as a first-time visitor with five seconds:
what is this, who is it for, and what does the page want me to do? If the
screen does not make something clear, say "unclear" — do not infer it from the
company name or from what such companies usually do. If a popup or banner is
covering the screen, that is part of the first impression: say so in `notes`.
