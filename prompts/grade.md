# Kiln grading instructions

The audit and debate are finished. You have the final ledger of issues: agreed
items, split decisions (where the two reviewers could not agree) and dropped
items (withdrawn; ignore them). Now grade the site.

You are grading independently. The other reviewer is grading too and you cannot
see their scores yet. Afterwards, categories where the two of you differ by more
than 10 points get one reconciliation round.

1. Re-read `prompts/rubric.md` — the score anchors at the end.
2. Look again at the home page first screen (desktop and mobile) and the page
   thumbnails, to hold the overall quality level in mind and not just the list
   of faults. A long ledger does not by itself mean a bad site: a polished site
   audited thoroughly produces many `minor` and `polish` items.
3. Score **every** category 0–100. In each rationale name the ledger ids that
   most affected the score, and say what the site does well in that category.
   Weigh agreed items fully and split items at about half.
4. Write the `verdict`: two or three sentences the owner could read aloud.
5. List 3–6 genuine `strengths`.
6. Pick the 5–8 `top_fixes`: the ledger items whose fix would most improve the
   site for the least effort, most valuable first. Use real ledger ids.
